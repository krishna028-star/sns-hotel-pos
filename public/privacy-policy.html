Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 9, 2025 · Effective: April 9, 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how SNS Hotels Group ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and shares information about you when you use the SNS Hotels POS application (the "App").

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly:

  • Account information: Name, email address, and role assigned by your hotel.
  • Order data: Food and beverage orders placed through the system.
  • Booking data: Table reservations including date, time, and guest count.
  • Payment data: Payment method type (Cash, UPI, Card) and transaction amount. We do not store card numbers — payments are processed by Razorpay.
  • Usage data: Timestamps, IP addresses, and actions logged for audit purposes.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and operate the POS service
  • To process food orders and payments
  • To manage table bookings and reservations
  • To track inventory and prevent theft
  • To generate financial and operational reports
  • To send operational alerts (KOT alarms, payment alerts, theft reports)
  • To maintain audit trails for security and compliance

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with:

  • Razorpay: Payment processing (subject to Razorpay's privacy policy)
  • Sentry: Error tracking and performance monitoring
  • AWS S3: Storage of inventory evidence images (if used)
  • Hotel management: Order, booking, and inventory data is accessible to authorized staff within your hotel and franchise chain.

4. Data Retention

We retain:

  • Order and payment records for 7 years (legal/accounting requirement)
  • Audit logs for 2 years
  • Account data until account deletion is requested

5. Security

We protect your information using:

  • HTTPS encryption (TLS 1.2+) for all data in transit
  • HTTP-only, SameSite cookies for session tokens
  • Role-based access control — staff only see data relevant to their role
  • Regular automated database backups with 30-day retention

6. Your Rights

You may request:

  • Access to your personal data
  • Correction of inaccurate data
  • Deletion of your account and associated data (subject to legal retention requirements)

To exercise these rights, contact: privacy@snshotels.com

7. Cookies

We use one essential cookie (sns_session) to maintain your login session. This cookie is HTTP-only and not accessible to JavaScript. We do not use tracking or advertising cookies.

8. Children's Privacy

This application is intended for use by hotel staff and adult customers. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. We will notify affected users by email and update the "Last updated" date above.
